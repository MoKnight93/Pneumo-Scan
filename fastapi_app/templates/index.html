Disease Overview

Understanding Pneumonia

Pneumonia is a serious lung infection affecting millions worldwide. Chest X-ray imaging is one of the main tools clinicians use to help diagnose it.

Clinical Reference

Causes, Symptoms & Prevention

A comprehensive overview of pneumonia's key clinical characteristics for educational reference.

Common Causes

  • Streptococcus pneumoniae — most common bacterial cause
  • Influenza virus and Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV)
  • Haemophilus influenzae — especially in children
  • Mycoplasma pneumoniae — "walking pneumonia"
  • Aspiration of food, liquids or stomach acid
  • Fungal infections in immunocompromised patients

Risk Factors

  • Age extremes — infants and elderly over 65
  • Chronic conditions: COPD, asthma, heart disease
  • Smoking — damages lung's natural defenses
  • Immunocompromised states: HIV, cancer chemotherapy
  • Hospitalization and mechanical ventilation
  • Malnutrition and poor living conditions

Symptoms & Signs

  • High fever, chills and rigors
  • Productive cough — may produce rust-colored sputum
  • Chest pain that worsens with breathing
  • Shortness of breath and rapid breathing
  • Fatigue and loss of appetite
  • Low oxygen saturation (<95%) on pulse oximetry

Prevention

  • Pneumococcal vaccine — highly effective in all ages
  • Annual influenza vaccination
  • Regular handwashing with soap and water
  • Avoid smoking and secondhand smoke exposure
  • Hib vaccine for children under 5 years
  • Proper ventilation in living and work spaces
